Who We Are

Our Story

S&G Global Solutions Pvt. Ltd. was founded with a clear vision: to connect India's finest agricultural produce with international markets. We strategically source from across the rich agricultural landscapes of South India, delivering the finest pomegranates, grapes, and raisins from our headquarters in Sangli, Maharashtra.

We bring together deep local farming relationships, export expertise, and a commitment to quality at every stage of the supply chain. By working directly with farmers and APMC networks, we source produce at peak quality and manage everything from grading and packaging to end-to-end logistics.

Our goal is simple — to be a trusted bridge between India's agricultural heartland and global markets, delivering premium produce with consistency, integrity, and care.
